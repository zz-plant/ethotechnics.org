# Copywriting plain-language audit (2026-02)

This audit flags copy that may confuse first-time visitors. Focus areas: jargon, abstract phrasing,
and terms that assume policy or governance background.

## Highest-friction copy to simplify

- **Current:** "...strengthen appeal and recourse..."  
  **Why it may confuse:** "recourse" is policy jargon; many users read this as legal language.  
  **Suggested rewrite:** "...make it easy for people to appeal decisions and get help when outcomes are
  wrong."
- **Current:** "...deploy mechanisms that connect delivery, research, and governance into enforceable
  action teams can defend."  
  **Why it may confuse:** too dense; "mechanisms" and "enforceable action" are abstract for newcomers.  
  **Suggested rewrite:** "...use practical steps that align product, research, and governance so teams can
  make decisions they can clearly explain."
- **Current:** "Spec-ready mechanisms and facilitation prompts."  
  **Why it may confuse:** "spec-ready" and "facilitation prompts" feel internal and process-heavy.  
  **Suggested rewrite:** "Templates you can paste into product specs, plus meeting prompts."
- **Current:** "Workshops, audits, and embedded co-delivery."  
  **Why it may confuse:** "embedded co-delivery" is unclear to non-consulting audiences.  
  **Suggested rewrite:** "Workshops, audits, and hands-on support inside your team."
- **Current:** "...verify decision receipts, clocks, and reversal authority."  
  **Why it may confuse:** "receipts," "clocks," and "reversal authority" are unfamiliar terms without
  context.  
  **Suggested rewrite:** "...confirm users get a decision record, know deadlines, and can reach someone
  who can reverse a wrong decision."
- **Current:** "A compact PDF pull from the mechanisms catalog..."  
  **Why it may confuse:** "PDF pull" is uncommon phrasing and may sound technical.  
  **Suggested rewrite:** "A short PDF excerpt from the mechanisms catalog..."
- **Current:** "A map linking diagnostics, the mechanisms catalog, and Studio pathways."  
  **Why it may confuse:** "Studio pathways" is brand-internal language.  
  **Suggested rewrite:** "A map linking diagnostics, practical guides, and optional Studio support."
- **Current:** "...score risk across data stewardship, consent, and downstream impact."  
  **Why it may confuse:** "data stewardship" and "downstream impact" are abstract for non-specialists.  
  **Suggested rewrite:** "...rate risk around data handling, user consent, and real-world impact after
  launch."

## Editorial pattern to apply site-wide

- Prefer concrete verbs over umbrella nouns (for example, "run a check" over "deploy a mechanism").
- Add a plain-language parenthetical on first use of domain terms (for example, "recourse (how users
  challenge a decision)").
- Replace internal service labels with outcomes (for example, "hands-on support" over "co-delivery").
