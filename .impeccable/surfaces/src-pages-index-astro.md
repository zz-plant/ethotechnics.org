# Home page surface brief

## Scope and mode

`/` — Read. A code-led editorial research front page. The owner authorized a substantial homepage
revamp, explicitly overriding maintenance; the completion request carries that authorization.
Product truth remains in `PRODUCT.md`. The design spec is

## Audience, job, action

One reader in three situations: a person an automated system decided about, someone who operates or
builds one, and someone who audits, regulates, or studies one. The reader should understand how
hidden human work, correction of rules, and continued capacity to act fit together, then follow the
relevant research or instrument. The opening action is “What Ethotechnics adds.” A visible triage
link serves a failure already in progress. The existing six-question self-test and six reader routes
remain further down the page.

## Proof and constraints

Robodebt illustrates the gap between correcting individual debts and changing the rule. The account
uses the existing casebook facts and links to sources. Research, draft standards, and the decision
record checker have separate reading routes and explicit limits. Drafts have no legal force; a
clean decision log does not establish a well-run institution. No adoption, novelty, effectiveness,
or response-time claims are invented. Keep `#record`, `#mechanism`, and `#self-test` destinations.
The self-test is a self-reported check with answers kept in the browser and a shareable result link.

## Chosen direction

The editorial research front page: Instrument Serif thesis, rust italic continuation, a marginal
comparison of reported results and omitted human work, a forest green evidence passage, and ruled
reading lists. Preserve the existing shared navigation and current project branding. No approved
image composition or new raster asset is part of this code-led surface.

The memorable opening is the question “Who keeps the system working—and can they make it change?”
The companion comparison uses recognizable human work without invented measurements.

## Direction contract

who can change the rule and what keeps them able to act.

regular and italic for homepage display; platform serif prose and platform sans controls. A square
forest green evidence section uses cream text. Fine rules and asymmetric columns organize reading.
Existing rounded navigation and tool controls retain their shared styles. Concern rows pair a
heading and explanation with a research link; they carry no redundant subject label. Arrows are
Lucide SVG icons.

STORY: Thesis and definition; three linked concerns; Robodebt and the casebook; separate routes into
theory, draft requirements, and record checks; self-test; reader routes; authorship, correction, and
commissioned-work information.

compact marginal comparison. A ruled lower band carries the definition, contribution action, and
triage route. At 900px and below, the comparison hides and columns stack. No fixed-height composition
is promised; what fits depends on viewport and text sizing.
