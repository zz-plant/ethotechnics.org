# Home page surface brief

## Scope and mode

`/` — Persuade. The visitor decides whether this project is worth their attention and takes one
step: mark their own system on the decision chain.

## Audience, job, action

One reader in three situations: a person an automated system decided about, someone who operates or
builds one, and someone who audits, regulates, or studies one. Job: recognize their own system in
the seven-stage chain and find the instrument that fits. Primary action: answer the six-question
self-test. Secondary: triage a failure in progress.

## Proof and constraints

Robodebt (about 470,000 unlawful debts, dozens of tribunal losses, three years and four months to
halt) and four more scored cases. Draft standards, record formats, and test instruments. No adoption
claims, no invented metrics, no response-time promises. Keep the existing copy claims, data, and
links. The visual world is open; the logo glyph and the name stay.

## Chosen direction

The Line: the seven-stage decision chain as a metropolitan transit diagram. Bright ground, black
structure, seven stage colours, station codes, interchange rings, a dashed reconsideration branch, a
black terminus, a legend, and a halt clock.

Memorable moment: a signal travels the chain and stops at the first station the reader's system
cannot answer; that station floods with its line colour and the halt clock starts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The decision chain is a transit line, not an essay. The page refuses the editorial hero and
shows the line, the station where correction stopped, and where the reader's system sits.

OWN-WORLD: A bright transit-diagram ground (`#f7f7f4`), near-black structure (`#111318`), and seven
stage colours — teal, cobalt, violet, vermilion, amber, magenta, ink — with a dashed magenta
reconsideration branch and a black correction terminus. Public Sans, self-hosted, set as signage:
heavy, tight, uppercase for station names; tabular codes in mono. Thin black rules, station dots,
interchange rings, a legend, and a halt clock. No serif, no cream, no card grid.

STORY: The visitor sees the seven stations, sees Robodebt's correction stop at Consequence, marks
their own system, gets a readout, and follows the line to the instrument that fixes the missing
station.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width horizontal line with seven stations across the page. The headline is a
station sign: "WHO HAS TO CHANGE IT?" The Robodebt fact sits beside the line as a service
disruption. A "Where is your system?" control sits on the line; the primary action, "Mark your
system", anchors to the self-test. A halt clock reads 3 yr 4 mo at the failed station.

FORM: Transit diagram, position 3 of 7 grounded worlds, seed key a7a4724b.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the
verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
