---
title: "What is a repair log?"
description: "A repair log records what was done to fix a harm or an error, with the receipt and the owner for each fix."
permalink: "/explainers/repair-log"
eyebrow: "Explainer"
glossaryRef: "repair-log"
definition:
  - "A repair log records, in order, each action taken to fix a harm or an error a system caused. Each entry links to the receipt, names the owner, and shows whether the repair is finished. Corrections stay visible and can be audited."
  - "Teams use it to show that remedies happened, and to see when the same failure keeps coming back."
example: "After an automated account lock is reversed, the repair log records the reversal, the compensation paid, and how long the remedy took."
distinctSignals:
  - title: "What each entry records"
    detail: "For each incident: what the harm was, what was done, who owns it, when the person was told, and how the fix was checked."
  - title: "Open until fixed"
    detail: "A postmortem with no follow-up ends up in an archive. Keep each entry open until the fix is confirmed in production."
  - title: "Proof that people were repaired"
    detail: "Publish the counts of open and closed repairs, how often incidents repeat, and proof that the people affected received a remedy or compensation."

faqs:
  - question: "Who owns the repair log?"
    answer: "The operator or steward responsible for remedy decisions, not the support queue."
  - question: "What does a repeat incident show?"
    answer: "That the earlier fix repaired the case and left the cause in place. Repeats under one rule go to that rule's owner, who changes the rule or records why not."
