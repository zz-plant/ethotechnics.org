title: "What is a Repair Log?"
description: "Repair logs track remediation actions after harm or errors and tie them to receipts and owners."

eyebrow: "Explainer"
glossaryRef: "repair-log"
definition:
  - "A repair log is a chronological record of remediation actions taken after a system causes harm or errors. It links each repair to a receipt, owner, and resolution status so corrections remain visible and auditable."
  - "Teams use repair logs to prove that remedies happened and to detect recurring failure patterns."
example: "After an automated account lock is reversed, the repair log records the reversal, compensation details, and the time-to-remedy clock."
distinctSignals:
  - title: "Repair schema"
    detail: "For each incident, capture harm statement, corrective action, owner, customer notification timestamp, and closure verification."
  - title: "Common failure pattern"
    detail: "Postmortems without follow-through decay into archives; require status transitions until remediation is validated in production."
  - title: "Evidence to ship"
    detail: "Expose open-versus-closed repair counts, repeat-incident rates, and proof that affected users received remedy or compensation."
