title: "What is a Burden Index?"
description: "A burden index quantifies the time and effort a system imposes on people."
permalink: "/explainers/burden-index"
eyebrow: "Explainer"
glossaryRef: "burden-index"
definition:
  - "A burden index quantifies the time and effort a system imposes on people to comply, appeal, or recover. It translates friction into a measurable metric so teams can reduce the time tax they create."
  - "Publishing the burden index keeps the human cost visible and accountable over time."
example: "A benefits portal tracks total minutes spent per appeal, publishes a monthly burden index, and commits to reducing the index quarter over quarter."
distinctSignals:
  - title: "Operational fingerprint"
    detail: "Define a per-journey burden scorecard (minutes, retries, documents requested) and publish it monthly so searchers can compare programs over time."
  - title: "Common failure pattern"
    detail: "Burden indices fail when teams average away outliers; include p90 and p99 burden to expose who experiences the longest administrative drag."
  - title: "Evidence to ship"
    detail: "Attach raw journey timing logs, abandonment points, and remediation commits that explain quarter-over-quarter burden reductions."
