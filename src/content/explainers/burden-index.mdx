title: "What is a burden index?"
description: "A burden index measures the minutes, retries, and documents a system demands from people who comply, appeal, or recover."
permalink: "/explainers/burden-index"
eyebrow: "Explainer"
glossaryRef: "burden-index"
definition:
  - "A burden index counts the time and effort a system demands of people who comply with it, appeal against it, or recover from it. It turns friction into a number a team can be held to."
  - "Published on a schedule, the index shows whether the cost to people rose or fell since the last report."
example: "A benefits portal tracks the total minutes each appeal takes. It publishes a burden index each month and commits to lowering it each quarter."
distinctSignals:
  - title: "A scorecard for each journey"
    detail: "Count the minutes, retries, and documents requested on each journey, so readers can compare one program with another."
  - title: "The people who wait longest"
    detail: "An average hides the people who spend the most time. Report p90 and p99 burden to show who spends longest on the paperwork."
  - title: "The logs behind each drop"
    detail: "Attach the raw journey timings, the points where people gave up, and the changes that explain each drop in the index."

    answer: "At least monthly for systems with high impact, with extra checks during peak volume or an incident."
