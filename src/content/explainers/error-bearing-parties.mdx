title: "Error-bearing parties"
description: "The people who absorb a system's errors are the first to see failures and the last able to act, unless standing gives them a route in."
permalink: "/explainers/error-bearing-parties"
eyebrow: "Explainer"
glossaryRef: "error-bearing-party"
definition:
  - "An error-bearing party is anyone who carries the consequences of a system's errors: the people its decisions fall on, and the staff whose corrective work keeps the system usable."
  - "Naming them comes before giving them standing. If the people exposed to errors have no route into the system, the institution has chosen not to hear from them."
example: "A benefits eligibility system lists two error-bearing parties for each decision class: the claimant whose payment is withheld, and the caseworker who spends unrecorded hours reversing claims put in the wrong category. Both can raise a challenge through the same route."
distinctSignals:
  - title: "A register that includes staff"
    detail: "The standing register names parties by role for each decision class, including internal staff. Each one can reach the system's own challenge route without going through a manager."
  - title: "Only outside users counted"
    detail: "Only external users are counted. The work of handling exceptions, which keeps the system looking reliable, stays invisible. The institution never sees the failure rate it is absorbing."
  - title: "Corrective work on the record"
    detail: "Report the share of decision classes with at least one named error-bearing party, and how much corrective work was recorded against the system rather than absorbed by staff."

faqs:
  - question: "Why include staff as error-bearing parties?"
    answer: "Because the system depends on their corrective work to stay usable. If that work is not counted, the institution reads a reliability figure that other people are paying for."
  - question: "Does standing mean a veto?"
    answer: "No. It means a challenge must be received, answered by a named person by a date, and able to change the decision. It does not have to win."
