title: "What is Contestability?"
description: "Contestability ensures people can challenge automated decisions and receive binding review."
permalink: "/explainers/contestability"
eyebrow: "Explainer"
glossaryRef: "contestability"
definition:
  - "Contestability is the ability for affected people to challenge and reverse automated decisions. It requires notice, a clear appeal path, and binding authority to change outcomes."
  - "Contestability turns accountability into a workflow with receipts, timelines, and documented remedies."
example: "An eligibility denial includes an appeal button that guarantees a human review within 72 hours and provides a receipt for every step."
distinctSignals:
  - title: "Decision object requirements"
    detail: "Specify the minimum fields in a contestable decision object: reason code, evidence source, owner, deadline, and reversal power."
  - title: "Common failure pattern"
    detail: "Appeal links without binding authority create performative review; name who can reverse outcomes and publish its deadline."
  - title: "Evidence to ship"
    detail: "Report reversal rate by decision type, median time-to-remedy, and percentage of appeals receiving complete receipts."
