      <section class="standard-section">
        <h2>Article I: The Right to Notice & Reasons</h2>
        <p class="standard-article-quote">
          "No decision shall be final without specific, actionable reasons."
        </p>
        <ol class="standard-clauses">
          <li>
            <span class="standard-clause-id">§1.1</span>
            Decision Objects: Every automated decision must produce a decision object containing reason codes, evidence sources, and scope boundaries.
          </li>
          <li>
            <span class="standard-clause-id">§1.2</span>
            Plain Language: Reasons must be written in language a layperson can understand. Legalese and internal codes are insufficient.
          </li>
          <li>
            <span class="standard-clause-id">§1.3</span>
            Immediate Availability: Reasons must be available at the moment of decision. Deferred explanation is not compliance.
          </li>
        </ol>
      </section>

      <section class="standard-section">
        <h2>Article II: The Right to an Appeal Path</h2>
        <p class="standard-article-quote">
          "The decision surface must include the appeal entry point."
        </p>
        <ol class="standard-clauses">
          <li>
            <span class="standard-clause-id">§2.1</span>
            Accessible Intake: The appeal path must be reachable directly from the decision notification or interface.
          </li>
          <li>
            <span class="standard-clause-id">§2.2</span>
            Named Authority: The appeal path must identify the accountable authority with reversal power.
          </li>
          <li>
            <span class="standard-clause-id">§2.3</span>
            Binding Deadlines: Appeal deadlines must be published, time-bound, and enforced.
          </li>
        </ol>
      </section>

      <section class="standard-section">
        <h2>Article III: The Right to a Review Clock</h2>
        <p class="standard-article-quote">
          "Unbounded review is a governance failure."
        </p>
        <ol class="standard-clauses">
          <li>
            <span class="standard-clause-id">§3.1</span>
            Declared Maximum: Every review process must declare a maximum duration.
          </li>
          <li>
            <span class="standard-clause-id">§3.2</span>
            Auto-Escalation: When the clock expires without resolution, the case must auto-escalate to a higher authority or default to reversal.
          </li>
          <li>
            <span class="standard-clause-id">§3.3</span>
            Interim Protections: While review is pending, the affected party must receive provisional protection (continued access, status quo, safe fallback).
          </li>
        </ol>
      </section>

      <section class="standard-section">
        <h2>Article IV: The Right to Remedy & Reversal</h2>
        <p class="standard-article-quote">
          "Reversal restores; compensation acknowledges."
        </p>
        <ol class="standard-clauses">
          <li>
            <span class="standard-clause-id">§4.1</span>
            Reversal as Default: Upheld appeals result in reversal of the contested decision unless safety criteria explicitly prohibit it.
          </li>
          <li>
            <span class="standard-clause-id">§4.2</span>
            Automatic Compensation: Wrongful harm triggers automatic credits, fee waivers, or restitution without requiring a separate claim.
          </li>
          <li>
            <span class="standard-clause-id">§4.3</span>
            Verified Delivery: Remedy delivery is tracked with timestamps and verified by the affected party.
          </li>
        </ol>
      </section>

      <section class="standard-section">
        <h2>Article V: The Right to Evidence & Accountability</h2>
        <p class="standard-article-quote">
          "If it isn't logged, it didn't happen."
        </p>
        <ol class="standard-clauses">
          <li>
            <span class="standard-clause-id">§5.1</span>
            Decision Receipts: Every decision emits a receipt with timestamp, owner, and decision object reference.
          </li>
          <li>
            <span class="standard-clause-id">§5.2</span>
            Outcome Transparency: Appeal passage rates and reversal rates are published and used to improve decisions.
          </li>
          <li>
            <span class="standard-clause-id">§5.3</span>
            Audit Trails: Audit trails link decisions to evidence, reviewers, and outcomes for every contested case.
          </li>
        </ol>
      </section>
